OmniLab Privacy

Current public release

Privacy, without the mystery.

This notice explains what the current OmniLab website sends, stores, and may measure when you explore a predicted reaction.

Updated July 24, 2026

At a glance

  • No account or payment details are required.
  • Chemical names leave your browser only when you request an analysis.
  • Your saved lab setup and returned result stay in your browser storage.

Reaction analysis

When you select Analyze Chemical Reaction, OmniLab sends the names of the selected chemicals to its server. The server matches that pair against OmniLab's defined reaction matrix to return an educational equation, explanation, and safety guidance. The input is intended for chemical names, so please don't enter personal, confidential, or identifying information.

Browser storage

OmniLab uses your browser's local storage to remember the selected chemicals, liquid appearance, and most recent reaction result on that device. Resetting the lab removes the saved setup, and a later analysis can replace the saved result. Your browser settings can clear all of this data.

Service data

The hosting and network services that deliver OmniLab may process standard request information, such as an IP address, browser details, and timestamps. The current public release has no account, payment, or profile system.

Product analytics

If limited product analytics are enabled, OmniLab may record a page visit, the first lab setup, and whether a reaction analysis starts, completes, or fails. These events are designed not to include selected chemical names or generated reaction content, and session recording and automatic click capture are disabled.

Contact messages

If you use the contact form, OmniLab sends your name, email address, optional subject, and message through its server to the managed support mailbox. This information is used to read and reply to your request. Please don't include sensitive information you don't want processed for that purpose.

Questions

For a privacy question, use the contact form.
